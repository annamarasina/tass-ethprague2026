Swarm Verified Fetch

Trust no gateway.

Lightweight verification for legal and security intelligence.

Why

  • SolidScan relies on external legal compliance and exploit intelligence.
  • We could not blindly trust public gateway responses.
  • Incorrect or tampered data would affect smart contract risk analysis.
  • We needed decentralized verification without running a full Bee node.

How

  • Implemented Swarm Verified Fetch as our backend trust layer.
  • Verified fetched JSON and legal datasets client-side with Swarm hashing.
  • Used Swarm for compliance intelligence, exploit history, and legal analysis.
  • Fetched from any gateway, then verified integrity locally before use.
